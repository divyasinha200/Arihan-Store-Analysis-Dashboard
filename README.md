# 🛍️ Arihant Store Sales Analysis Dashboard

An Excel-based dashboard project for analyzing Arihant Store's sales and order trends. The goal was to uncover valuable business insights from raw data and enable better decision-making through clean visualizations.

---

## 👩‍💼 Business Analyst Role

### 🧩 Business Problem

Arihant Store had a large volume of customer and sales data but lacked a clear understanding of *who their best customers were, **which regions were performing, and **why certain orders were being canceled or returned*. Leadership needed a quick, visual way to monitor trends and make smarter decisions about marketing, inventory, and fulfillment.

---

### 💡 My Role as a Business Analyst

As a Business Analyst, I:

- Identified key business questions:
  - Which states are driving the most revenue?
  - What is the return/cancellation rate?
  - Is one gender purchasing more?
  - Which sales channel performs best?

- Defined clear KPIs like:
  - Total Orders, Total Sales, Sales by Gender/Region/Channel
  - % Delivered vs Canceled vs Returned

- Designed dashboards with visuals that directly answer the above

- Derived business recommendations based on analysis

---

### 🔍 Insights & Recommendations

- 👨‍👩‍👧 *Women accounted for more orders than men*, especially in younger age groups  
  ➤ Recommended gender-targeted marketing campaigns

- 📦 *Highest order cancellation rate was through one specific sales channel*  
  ➤ Suggested reviewing logistics of that channel

- 🌐 *Most revenue came from online orders, not in-store*  
  ➤ Suggested investing more in digital advertising

- 🗺️ *Top-performing states contributed over 50% of revenue*  
  ➤ Recommended prioritizing those states in sales campaigns

---

### 📈 Business Impact

- Helped leadership spot underperforming channels
- Supported targeted campaigns by customer segment
- Increased clarity around customer behavior and regional performance

## 👩‍💻 Data Analyst Role

### 🛠 Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel  
  - Pivot Tables  
  - Charts  
  - Filters  
  - Dashboard Design  

---

### 🔧 What I Did as a Data Analyst

- Cleaned raw sales and customer data  
- Built calculated columns for KPIs (Total Orders, Revenue, Gender Distribution, etc.)  
- Created Pivot Tables to explore:
  - Orders by Gender, Age, Region
  - Sales by Channel
  - Order Status breakdown

- Designed a dashboard with:
  - Clustered column charts
  - Pie charts for gender and status
  - Bar charts for regional performance
  - Slicers for month/channel filtering
